### Here, only the effect of the circular band in strontium ruthenate is considered. The circular band couples very weakly to STM due to the in-plane nature of the $xy$ orbitals constituting the band.




`cuprate_julia_code_strontium_ruthenate_BdG_lattice.ipynb`: Generates lattice Green's functions for $d_{x^2 - y^2}$ superconductor in strontium ruthenate.

`cuprate_Wannier.ipynb`: Convolutes the lattice level data for $d_{x^2 - y^2}$ superconductor with Wannier function.
