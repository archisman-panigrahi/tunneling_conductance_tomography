### Here, the combined effect of the two square bands in strontium ruthenate is considered. These bands are out-of plane in nature, and couple strongly with the STM tip.



`cuprate_xzyz_julia_code_strontium_ruthenate_BdG_lattice.ipynb`: Generates lattice Green's functions for $d_{x^2 - y^2}$ superconductor in strontium ruthenate.

`cuprate_Wannier.ipynb`: Convolutes the lattice level data for $d_{x^2 - y^2}$ superconductor with Wannier function.

---

`d+id_xzyz_julia_code_strontium_ruthenate_BdG_lattice.ipynb`: Generates lattice Green's functions for $d+id$ superconductor in strontium ruthenate.

`d+id_Wannier.ipynb`: Convolutes the lattice level data for $d+id$ superconductor with Wannier function.

---

`p+ip_xzyz_julia_code_strontium_ruthenate_BdG_lattice.ipynb: Generates lattice Green's functions for $p+ip$ superconductor in strontium ruthenate.

`p+ip_Wannier.ipynb`: Convolutes the lattice level data for $p+ip$ superconductor with Wannier function.

---

`s_wave_xzyz_julia_code_strontium_ruthenate_BdG.ipynb`: Generates lattice Green's functions for $s$-wave superconductor in strontium ruthenate.
`s_wave_Wannier.ipynb`: Convolutes the lattice level data for $s$-wave superconductor with Wannier function.
